Monitor. Understand. Respond.

CarePulse delivers end-to-end telemetry from patient sensors to intuitive clinical dashboards with proactive alerts.

1. Real-Time Monitoring

Continuous telemetry of Heart Rate (BPM), Blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO₂), and Core Body Temperature (°C) with live oscilloscope ECG wave rendering.

2. Health Analytics

Interactive multi-day trend graphs, rolling averages, circadian baseline comparisons, and printer-ready medical summary reports.

3. Smart Alerts

Configurable project thresholds categorize vitals into Normal, Warning, and Critical states with intelligent alert deduplication and auditory chimes.
